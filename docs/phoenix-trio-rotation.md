## The likely misses, ranked

1. ~~Phoenix's skills.~~ **Ruled out 10/23:** Core F, Basic 12, Special 10, Chain/Ult 10, Assist 5, Dodge 2.
   That matches Lin-0's priority (Core > Basic > Ult > Special > Assist > Dodge). Next levels go to Chain/Ult → 12.
2. **Lineup/swap order: Remielle → Phoenix.** Remielle's Lumiflux takes the element of the *next* agent
   swapped in after her (Prydwen; also a score-2 Reddit comment). Her handoff should go to Phoenix.
3. **Don't Chain Attack *into* Remielle.** It skips her swap-in Assist (Prydwen). Give chains to
   **Phoenix**, which has the 2nd-highest Chain Attack anomaly buildup in the game (Lin-0): two Phoenix chains per stun.
4. **Phoenix Ult → press Basic immediately.** Ult burns her HP and enters Wither, and auto-Quick-Assists out.
   The payoff is **Soul-Searching Blaze** (an Abloom at 597% with Lv12 skills, +40% CRIT DMG), triggered by Basic,
   by swapping back, or after 3s. Don't wait on the animation. Time it so it lands *before stun ends*.
5. **Velina EX timing vs Phase Flow.** Fire Windswept *after* Remielle is confirmed in Phase Flow (~0.5 s
   beat). Too early erases her Voidflare stacks (Lin-0, single source, worth testing).
6. **Phoenix's loop.** EX1 → EX2 (grants Ascension) → **free EX3**, cancel into **Basic 3 → 4** (skip 1–2).
   Repeat to 90 Pyre, then **hold Basic** (Enhanced Basic). **Swap-cancel** out of it, because it keeps resolving off-field.
   Don't hold Special or fish for parries: the unparried Alt-Basic string builds more anomaly per second (120–132/s vs 110/s).
7. **Snapshot order.** Anomaly buildup snapshots buffs on application. Get Windswept and buffs up *before*
   laying Burn (Lin-0).
8. **Velina tornadoes.** Don't overwrite a tornado that's about to pop (2 dots + proc imminent → let it finish, then EX).
   With 0–1 dots → EX freely. Score-1 Reddit, unverified. Velina Ult when Wind gauge is low.
9. **The team is Vortex, not Disorder.** Sear CRIT doesn't touch Disorder. Velina converts cross-attribute pops
   into Vortex (which crits). Disorder is the leftover case.

## Sample stun rotation (Lin-0 "Blueprint A", Vortex-max)

1. Pre-stun: Phoenix EX1 → EX2 (Ascension). Velina EX to prime Wind near cap.
2. Stun: Phoenix free EX3 (Vortex #1) → B3 → B4 → swap-cancel to Remielle.
3. Remielle Entry → Ult → **chain prompt: pick Phoenix** (Vortex #2, Sweeping Cyclone → Windswept) → Phoenix EX1.
4. Mid-EX1, swap Velina → EX → chain prompt: Phoenix again → Enhanced Basic (Vortex #3), swap out while it resolves.
5. Remielle hold Basic → EX (Phase Flow). Swap Phoenix → Ult → Basic for Soul-Searching Blaze before stun ends (Vortex #4).

"Blueprint B" (Voidflare economy): same opener, but **cancel** the chain after Remielle's Ult, then hold Basic
→ EX back into Phase Flow. Then Velina EX (after the beat) → chain Phoenix → Enhanced Basic → Velina hold-EX
Quick-Assists Phoenix → Ult → Blaze.

  Three attributes = marks on tap.

  to break charges or prime before stun. EX queued in stun instead of basics. Resources pooled for a final-20s triple-Ult dump.
